## Outcome and scope

At owning boundaries in `extensions/delegate.ts`, `extensions/delegate-implement.ts`, and `extensions/guard.ts` as needed, expose the minimum trusted facts: source/owner and correlated job/attempt/tool-call; cause distinct from resource; denied/completed/partial/unknown effect; and delivered check/WIP/commit/integrity state. Preserve existing guards. Propagate a small run-scoped fallback restriction from the coordinator's recorded user-approved contract: frozen eligible models/providers and permitted task-data/destination scope. Missing or incompatible evidence disables fallback; a typed object or configuration does not authenticate consent.

Keep original absolute hard and tool-wrap-up deadlines and cumulative hard/tool cost allowances when changing candidates. Before any candidate restart, retain denial/integrity evidence and reconcile effects; unknown/unsafe effects return a handback, never replay. Use provider/runtime-owned structured classification, not model prose, generic regexes, or a shell command's `429` output.

Pi's supported `tool_execution_update` event carries `partialResult` (partial tool output), not an effect-state classification. The delegate correlates and records that observation separately while preserving `effect: unknown` on a failed call; it does not invent `effect: partial`. Partial output is not evidence of a partial side effect. Denied, completed, and unknown effects are handled conservatively: unknown or non-completed effects refuse replay and acceptance. The absence of a runtime partial-side-effect classification remains an explicit capability limit, not evidence that a partial effect occurred or permission to replay.

### Endpoint evidence correction (integrated Standards `bg83`)

The installed Pi 1.4.0 runtime does not make `ModelRegistry.find()`'s catalog `baseUrl` the effective request URL in all cases: `ModelRuntime.prepareRequest()` replaces it with `auth.baseUrl` from request-time auth, and GitHub Copilot's OAuth `toAuth()` derives a credential-specific endpoint. `ModelRegistry.getApiKeyAndHeaders()` resolves credentials and is not used for this check; the SDK exposes no credential-free effective-endpoint accessor. The GitLab Duo custom-provider example also changes the request model's base URL inside its stream implementation.

Fallback now accepts a catalog destination only for audited direct transports that use `model.baseUrl`, with known non-OAuth auth, and no registered provider extension or Azure endpoint override. Unknown/missing metadata, OAuth auth, extension providers, Azure, and non-audited transports fail closed even when the supplied destination matches the catalog URL. This intentionally limits fallback until Pi exposes an endpoint-only pinning interface; it does not treat model registry metadata as endpoint authentication or authorize new providers. The coordinator's model/provider/data scope, exact-model behavior, shared budgets, provenance and post-tool effect checks remain unchanged.

Evidence: installed `dist/core/model-runtime.js` (`prepareRequest`), `dist/core/model-registry.js` and `dist/core/model-registry.d.ts` (`find`, `getApiKeyAndHeaders`, `isUsingOAuth`, registered-provider metadata), `@earendil-works/pi-ai/dist/auth/oauth/github-copilot.js` (`toAuth`), and `examples/extensions/custom-provider-gitlab-duo/index.ts`. Relevant Pi docs read: `docs/models.md`, `docs/providers.md`, `docs/custom-provider.md`, `docs/models-configuration.md`, `docs/configuration.md`, `docs/extensions.md`, `docs/sdk.md`, and their model/auth/provider cross-references. This correction still requires its fresh repository check and independent affected-delta closure; A2 and A4 are not marked independently closed or active.

## Decided boundaries

- This is the intentional safety migration of legacy fallback authorization/classification, distinct from A5's backward-compatible review-pool selection. A5 may ship first with existing fallback behavior; after A2 all pools obey A2. A2 does not require A5.
- Exact-model instructions stay exact; configured pools alone do not authorize fallback. Quota/billing exhaustion is not transient availability. Unknown cause/effects fail closed for replay and acceptance.
- No telemetry subsystem, broad event schema, new consent service/UI, runtime ledger, automatic retry, account/provider/quota evasion, or malicious-code-sandbox claim.
- A2 may be implemented in small bounded slices through the owning interfaces/tests. Keep those slices within A2; they do not require new tickets, graph machinery, panels, or routine human approval. Preserve current stop-on-guard/worker-limit policy until A4.

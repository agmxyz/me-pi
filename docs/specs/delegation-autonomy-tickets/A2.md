## Outcome and scope

At owning boundaries in `extensions/delegate.ts`, `extensions/delegate-implement.ts`, and `extensions/guard.ts` as needed, expose the minimum trusted facts: source/owner and correlated job/attempt/tool-call; cause distinct from resource; denied/completed/partial/unknown effect; and delivered check/WIP/commit/integrity state. Preserve existing guards. Propagate a small run-scoped fallback restriction from the coordinator's recorded user-approved contract: frozen eligible models/providers and permitted task-data/destination scope. Missing or incompatible evidence disables fallback; a typed object or configuration does not authenticate consent.

Keep original absolute hard and tool-wrap-up deadlines and cumulative hard/tool cost allowances when changing candidates. Before any candidate restart, retain denial/integrity evidence and reconcile effects; unknown/unsafe effects return a handback, never replay. Use provider/runtime-owned structured classification, not model prose, generic regexes, or a shell command's `429` output.

## Current partial recovery

The recovery slice in this local commit records both `hard-cost-limit/hard-cost` and `wrap-up-limit/tool-cost` when a continuing reply exhausts both remaining cumulative allowances, before the hard-cost stop can terminate the worker and prevent `auto_retry_start`. The focused regression requires both facts, and this commit records the exact repository check result. This passing slice does **not** establish full A2 acceptance.

Remaining acceptance gap: Pi's supported `tool_execution_update` event carries `partialResult` (partial tool output), not an effect-state classification. The delegate now correlates and records that observation separately while preserving `effect: unknown` on a failed call; it does not invent `effect: partial`. The regression proves such evidence still refuses replay, and the implement acceptance gate refuses absent/incomplete provenance and unknown/non-completed effects even with a passing check. No owner-defined partial-side-effect fact is currently emitted at this boundary, so partial effects cannot be distinguished from unknown effects and remain an explicit source/acceptance limitation. Named/adopted coordinator contracts are tested as enforcement inputs, not as independently authenticated approvals. Final integrated independent Standards/Spec review remains pending.

## Decided boundaries

- This is the intentional safety migration of legacy fallback authorization/classification, distinct from A5's backward-compatible review-pool selection. A5 may ship first with existing fallback behavior; after A2 all pools obey A2. A2 does not require A5.
- Exact-model instructions stay exact; configured pools alone do not authorize fallback. Quota/billing exhaustion is not transient availability. Unknown cause/effects fail closed for replay and acceptance.
- No telemetry subsystem, broad event schema, new consent service/UI, runtime ledger, automatic retry, account/provider/quota evasion, or malicious-code-sandbox claim.
- A2 may be implemented in small bounded slices through the owning interfaces/tests. Keep those slices within A2; they do not require new tickets, graph machinery, panels, or routine human approval. Preserve current stop-on-guard/worker-limit policy until A4.

## Acceptance and test seams

Keep wrap-up distinct from parent hard timeout, cost/call dimensions, ordinary command failure, provider unavailability/quota, guard denial, locks, and integrity/tripwire. Retain competing safety causes beside a time stop. Missing/conflicting provenance cannot authorize replay. Test denied versus unknown/partial effects; pass/stale/none; normal/WIP commits; exact model; shared budgets; explicitly named authorization and adopted snapshots; eligible/ineligible candidates and destinations; absent authorization and later configuration changes; structured transient versus hard quota; failing shell `429` without provider fallback; and post-tool provider failure showing handback before unsafe restart. Prove fallback retains the original wrap-up ceiling and cumulative cost/time. Preserve cancellation, WIP, and tripwire coverage.
